Henley High School

Start your journey

Find out more

A student’s journey through school is one of life’s greatest and most important adventures. School guides, shapes and transforms. It allows students to explore – explore new ideas and concepts, new ways of thinking and feeling, new ways of being.

At Henley High School, our vision is to prepare our students to flourish in an evolving and challenging world. We value the school journey and are dedicated to helping each student grow and become their best self, able to adapt, respond and contribute as a resilient, ambitious and contemplative human being.
